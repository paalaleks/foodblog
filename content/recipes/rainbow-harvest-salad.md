A generous bowl of colour is a lovely way to make a simple meal feel special. Warm sweet potato meets crunchy leaves and a creamy dressing, with chickpeas to bring everything together.

Serve as a side for four, or divide into two larger bowls for lunch.

## Ingredients

- 1 medium sweet potato, cut into 2 cm cubes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 120 g lettuce leaves, washed and dried
- 1 tin chickpeas (400 g), drained and rinsed
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 150 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 100 g red cabbage, finely sliced
- 4 radishes, sliced
- 1 yellow pepper, sliced
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 3 tbsp water, plus more as needed
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Toss the sweet potato with olive oil and a pinch of salt. Spread on a baking tray and roast for 25 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and golden.
2. Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, maple syrup, and water in a small bowl. Add a little more water if needed for a pourable dressing. Season to taste.
3. Arrange the lettuce, chickpeas, avocado, tomatoes, cabbage, radishes, and pepper in a serving bowl. Add the warm sweet potato.
4. Spoon the dressing over just before serving and toss gently at the table.

## Notes

Keep the dressing separate if preparing ahead. Add the avocado shortly before serving. Tahini contains sesame; check guests' requirements before serving.
