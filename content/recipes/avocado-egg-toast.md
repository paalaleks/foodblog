A handful of good ingredients is all a morning needs. Choose an avocado that gives gently when pressed and bread with enough structure to hold its toppings.

## Ingredients

- 2 thick slices of sourdough bread
- 1 ripe avocado
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Toast the sourdough until crisp and golden.
2. Scoop the avocado into a bowl. Mash roughly with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt, keeping a little texture.
3. Heat the olive oil in a nonstick frying pan over medium-low heat. Crack in the eggs and fry for 4–6 minutes, or longer to your preferred doneness. Cover briefly to help the tops set.
4. Spread the avocado over the toast. Top each slice with an egg and finish with chilli flakes and black pepper. Serve straight away.

## Notes

For fully cooked eggs, cook until both the whites and yolks are firm. The avocado mixture is best made just before serving.
